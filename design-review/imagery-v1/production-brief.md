# Hello You Labs — image direction 01

**Status: approval only. No website changes authorized or applied.**

## What this board actually contains

The proposal uses three existing product-artwork files: `img/vials/semaglutide-5mg.png`, `img/vials/tirzepatide-10mg.png`, and `img/vials/bpc157-5mg.png`.

Their outer backgrounds were masked, the vials were rotated upright and resized, and the original pixels were composited over procedural gradients and geometric surface illustrations. Text on the vials was not regenerated, corrected, or replaced. No generative image model was available or used. These supplied images have not been verified as original camera photography, so the board calls them **supplied product artwork**, not original photography.

- **Source panel:** original supplied product artwork, resized for reference.
- **Desktop/mobile hero and brand story:** product composites with digitally illustrated architectural surfaces.
- **Catalog and selection:** product composites with gradients and simulated shadows.
- **Quality:** enlarged crop of a product cutout, composited over teal.
- **Shipping:** a photo-request card, not a proposed photograph or invented packaging.
- **Stock photography:** none added to this proposal.

These are composition and palette studies. The source glass retains dark transmitted/reflected pixels from its original setting. A cutout cannot authentically reconstruct glass photographed in a different environment. Enlargements cannot recover details absent from the supplied files. Some masking edges need manual retouching before production. Use a new product shoot for the strongest photographic result; do not present these studies as finished commercial photography.

## Original photos needed

### Product master photographs — highest priority

For every product to be featured, supply one uncompressed, sharp, front-facing photograph and one three-quarter view. Ideally use RAW or full-resolution camera files, at least 3000 pixels on the long side. Use the same camera position and diffused side lighting across the collection. Include the entire cap and base. Keep the label clean and fully readable. Avoid portrait-mode blur, direct flash, beauty filters, watermarks, and screenshots.

Photograph clear glass against both a neutral pale background and a dark background. These paired references help a retoucher handle edges and translucency without inventing product details. A transparent export is useful, but not a replacement for properly exposed glass photographs.

Confirm that photographed packaging is current. Existing vial artwork contains label claims; preserving them is not independent verification. Do not add or promote new strength, purity, accreditation, or certification claims based on the images.

### Shipping

1. Closed outer carton, three-quarter angle.
2. Open carton showing the actual insert and protective packing.
3. A genuine packed order before sealing.
4. Any actual temperature-control materials, **only if used for that shipment**.

Remove or cover customer names, addresses, tracking labels, order identifiers, and other personal information before sharing. Do not imply cold-chain shipping, special materials, or a branded box unless those are actually used.

### Optional team/workspace

Supply an authentic workspace photograph or consenting team-member photo if you want people in the brand story. Otherwise the product still life is the recommended replacement. Do not use a fabricated facility, AI employee, doctor, or patient as company evidence.

## Shared production instructions

Use the following with a photographer/retoucher, or with an image tool that supports controlled backgrounds and reference compositing:

> Produce restrained premium research-product imagery for Hello You Labs. Preserve the exact supplied vial, cap, logo, product name, label typography, and packaging. Treat the original product as a locked photographic layer: no generative changes inside the product silhouette and no replacement lettering. Do not generate vials from text alone. Create the environment separately if necessary, then composite the supplied product with physically plausible contact shadows. If accurate glass transmission cannot be preserved, request a new photograph instead of inventing it. Palette: deep teal #00474B, muted teal, ice blue #EDF6F7, pale neutral stone, and the existing black-and-gold packaging. Soft large-source side light, controlled metal highlights, subtle realistic surface texture. No invented product text, strengths, certifications, test results, or company facilities. No glowing molecules, floating products, excessive CGI, people, needles in use, treatment scenes, lens flares, or fake depth-of-field artifacts. Deliver a clean image without baked-in headline, buttons, captions, or watermark. Keep all supplied research-use wording intact.

### 01 — Desktop hero

> Create a landscape editorial product photograph, 2400 × 1200 pixels. Arrange the actual supplied Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, and BPC-157 vials on a simple pale limestone or matte plaster support, grouped within the rightmost 42% of the frame. Use normal real-world proportions; modest size variation should come from depth, not distorted products. Deep teal backdrop with a restrained soft falloff; keep the left 54% low-detail and consistently dark enough for live website text. Gentle upper-left daylight, clean black packaging, accurate warm-gold label accents, restrained metal reflections. Every vial must make believable physical contact with its supporting surface. Avoid a hovering base or visible undersurface inconsistent with the camera angle. Keep the front product label readable. Leave right-edge and bottom crop safety. No embedded headline. Use locked original product photography; if only a background can be generated, deliver the empty environment and a placement guide rather than inventing the products.

### 02 — Mobile hero

> Create a separate portrait composition, 1200 × 1920 pixels, using two actual Hello You vials. Use the same palette, lighting, and material treatment as the approved desktop image, but do not simply crop the desktop file. Provide generous quiet teal space across the upper frame for the existing mobile headline and controls. Keep the product group compact in the lower-right area and provide a version with extra lower margin. Include a placement proof with the existing mobile text and feature card as separate editable layers. Do not bake interface elements into the delivered image. Avoid putting important label information under the live interface. Final framing must be checked at 320, 375, 390, and 430 CSS pixels without moving or obscuring the existing controls.

### 03 — Catalog master and series

> Photograph or composite one actual Hello You vial centered in a square 1600 × 1600 frame. Use a pale blue-gray seamless background, slight tonal depth, and a soft shadow that meets the real base. Vial height should occupy approximately 70–74% of the frame, with comfortable cap and base clearance. Camera angle, product scale, white balance, background tone, and shadow direction must be identical across the series. Show a natural upright product, not a floating vial. Preserve the exact label from the supplied source; no AI-generated text or synthetic label replacement. Approve one master before producing the full 13-product series. Keep the image free of prices, category badges, invented concentrations, and interface text.

### 04 — Brand-story still life

> Create a 1600 × 1200 editorial still life with two actual Hello You vials, one on a low pale architectural block and one resting on the surrounding surface. Use a quiet ice-blue environment with a subtle soft window-light gradient. Clear physical contact, realistic glass and metal, clean negative space, no busy props. The scene should communicate care and attention through product presentation rather than an invented clinical setting. No laboratory machinery, employee, facility logo, certificates, or location claims. Preserve actual product pixels and labels. If genuine team or workspace photography is supplied later, produce a separate authentic-photo option for comparison.

### 05 — Ordering / product selection

> Produce a 1600 × 1200 product-selection still life. Place three actual Hello You research vials in an evenly spaced horizontal arrangement on a pale blue-gray surface, with matched camera angle and consistent scale. Keep label names legible. Soft broad side light, restrained shadows, no new labeling or paperwork. The arrangement should look like carefully selected laboratory materials, not a patient treatment plan or recommended combination for personal use. No syringes, dosing tools, patient props, or therapeutic claims. Composition must be visually distinct from the clustered hero.

### 06 — Quality macro

> Produce a 1400 × 1750 macro image from an actual high-resolution Hello You vial photograph, concentrating on the cap edge, glass neck, and upper label. Use a dark muted-teal environment and controlled highlights that reveal genuine metal and glass detail. Keep product geometry and text unchanged. Prefer a real macro capture to enlargement or generative reconstruction. The frame should imply attention to physical detail without claiming a test result, purity level, certification, manufacturing process, or regulatory approval. Do not invent extra label text. Preserve the existing product video as a separate asset; this image should complement it.

### 07 — Shipping — only after references arrive

> Photograph the actual Hello You shipping carton and its real packing configuration, using the supplied packaging references as the source of truth. Create a 1600 × 1200 three-quarter tabletop view with soft daylight, pale neutral background, and natural shadows. Show an orderly, believable packed research order with materials exactly as they are shipped. Do not add branded packaging, insulated liners, ice packs, safety seals, or paperwork that is not actually used. No invented COAs or test data. Exclude all customer and tracking information. Keep the carton visually distinct from the vial-focused images elsewhere on the page.
