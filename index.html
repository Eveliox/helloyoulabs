Ask our team
Are these products for human use?+

No. Every product is sold strictly for in-vitro laboratory research and development. Products are not intended for human or veterinary use, are not sold as medicines or supplements, and are not FDA-approved for any medicinal purpose.

How do I place an order?+

Choose your products and vial options, then open your cart and select “Continue on WhatsApp.” Your selections will be included in a message to our team. Availability, shipping, and payment are confirmed in the conversation. Online card checkout is not currently available.

Can I review a certificate of analysis?+

Yes. Use “Request COA” in a product's details, or message our team with the product name. Request the documentation for the current lot before ordering, and match the lot number on your received vial to the certificate.

Where do you ship?+

We ship domestically within the United States. The team will confirm available shipping services, costs, and any temperature-sensitive packaging requirements before you finalize your order.

What if there's a problem with delivery?+

Contact our team on WhatsApp with your order details. If your package or vial arrives damaged, keep the packaging and share photos within 72 hours so we can review the issue and help with next steps.

Do you offer wholesale ordering?+

For laboratory and bulk purchasing inquiries, visit our wholesale application page or contact our team to discuss your requirements and current availability.
