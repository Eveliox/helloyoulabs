FAQ

Straight
answers.

If a question isn't answered here, email support@helloyoulabs.example.

Are these products for human use?
No. All products sold by Hello You Labs are supplied strictly for in-vitro laboratory research and development use. Products are not intended for human or veterinary consumption, are not approved by the FDA for any medicinal purpose, and are not sold as drugs, dietary supplements, or cosmetics.
How do I verify a COA?
Each shipment includes a printed or PDF certificate of analysis corresponding to the exact lot number on your vial. Cross-check the lot number on the vial label with the "Lot" field on the COA; the two must match exactly. If they do not, do not use the material — contact support and we will replace the order.
What payment methods do you accept?
Standard credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover). We do not require cryptocurrency. We do not store card numbers on our servers; all payment processing is handled by a PCI-compliant third-party gateway.
Do you ship internationally?
No. Hello You Labs ships to addresses in the fifty United States only. International orders and freight-forwarder addresses are declined at checkout to remain compliant with US export controls and destination-country regulations.
What if my product arrives damaged?
Photograph the outer carton and vial as received and email support within 72 hours of delivery. Verified damage or transit failure (broken vial, expired cold-pack) is replaced free of charge from the same lot when inventory permits, or refunded in full.
How are peptides stored and shipped?
Lyophilized peptides are stable short-term at ambient temperature and are stored long-term at 2–8 °C sealed and desiccated. For temperature-sensitive lots we ship with insulated liners and gel ice packs and prioritize 2-day carrier service. After reconstitution for in-vitro assays, refer to the lot-specific COA for stability guidance.
